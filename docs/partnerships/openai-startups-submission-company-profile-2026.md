**What does your company do?**
Trends172Tech designs, develops and deploys customized business software with artificial intelligence. Our engineering is grounded in real operational deployments, rather than prototypes alone. CarpiHogar is our flagship commerce and ERP implementation, integrating inventory, sales, purchasing, marketing, messaging, APIs, identity and AI-assisted workflows. The reusable LUNA framework and modular approach let us adapt this engineering into additional products and client solutions.
